Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sweet Merge handles information when you use the game.

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

Sweet Merge may include advertising, in-app purchases and analytics. The exact third-party providers used by the current version of the game should also be reflected in the Google Play Data Safety form.

1. Information We Collect

Sweet Merge does not require users to directly provide personal information such as a name, home address or phone number in order to play.

The game and third-party services integrated into it may automatically process limited technical and usage information, including:

  • Device type, model, operating system and app version
  • Language and general device settings
  • Device or advertising identifiers where permitted
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information
  • Gameplay activity, level progress, sessions and in-game interactions
  • Crash logs, diagnostic and performance information
  • Purchase status, product information and transaction-related identifiers

2. Analytics

Sweet Merge uses analytics technologies to understand how players interact with the game and to improve gameplay, stability, balancing and performance.

Analytics services may process gameplay events, session information, device information, app version, crash data and anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers.

3. Advertising

Sweet Merge may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners.

Advertising providers may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, ad impressions, clicks, interactions and app usage data for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

Where required by law, consent will be requested before information is used for personalized advertising.

4. In-App Purchases

Sweet Merge may offer optional digital products, virtual items, currencies or other content through in-app purchases.

Payments are processed through Google Play Billing or the applicable app store payment system. MageCat Games does not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

Limited transaction information may be processed to deliver purchased content, validate purchases, restore eligible purchases, prevent fraud and provide support.

5. Third-Party Services

Sweet Merge may use third-party services for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, payment processing, game functionality, cloud services and app distribution.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. The exact services used may change as the game is updated.

Where applicable, the current providers and data practices will also be reflected in the Google Play Data Safety section.

6. How We Use Information

  • Operate and maintain Sweet Merge
  • Provide gameplay features and purchased content
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Analyze gameplay and improve the user experience
  • Diagnose crashes and technical issues
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Limited information may be shared with trusted service providers where necessary for advertising, analytics, payments, crash reporting, hosting, platform services, security or legal compliance.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through Sweet Merge. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required by law. Third-party providers may retain data according to their own retention policies.

10. User Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with you.

Sweet Merge does not require a traditional player account unless otherwise stated inside the game. You may contact us to request information about or deletion of data that may be associated with your use of the game.

Some records, including certain purchase or fraud-prevention records, may need to be retained where required by law or platform rules.

11. Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. Sweet Merge does not knowingly ask children to directly submit personal information through the game.

If the game is made available to children, we will take appropriate measures to comply with applicable laws and platform requirements relating to children and families.

12. International Data Processing

Some third-party service providers may process information on servers located outside your country of residence. Where applicable, such processing is handled in accordance with relevant legal requirements and the policies of those providers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to changes in Sweet Merge, third-party services, legal requirements or our data practices. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will be changed when this policy is updated.
